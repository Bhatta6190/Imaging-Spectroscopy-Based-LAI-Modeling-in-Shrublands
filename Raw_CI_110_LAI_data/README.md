## CI-110 Based LAI Data Collection in Fynbos

Raw CI-110 field data is added here to document the original LAI measurements collected at Grootbos. The raw dataset includes CI-110 Plant Canopy Analyzer readings collected approximately 10 cm above the ground, with up to five canopy readings per site: one at the plot center and four in the cardinal directions, approximately 1.5 m from the center. The dataset includes both PAR-based LAI and hemispherical gap-fraction LAI outputs; however, our manuscript uses the gap-fraction LAI values because the PAR-based estimates were considered less reliable under variable illumination and patchy cloud conditions. Site photographs, including images with a 2 m ranging rod, are also included to support interpretation of vegetation height and structure. Some hemispherical images were reprocessed using a 60 degree zenith ending angle to reduce contamination from operators appearing near the outer image ring.

The raw file names follow this convention: `GBS` = Grootbos, `06/16/17/19/20` = burn year, `P` = fixed survey plot, numeric values = random-point identifiers, and the final suffix such as `_1`, `_2`, or `_3` = replicate number. For example, `GBS06P_2` refers to replicate 2 from the fixed survey plot in the 2006 burn block, while `GBS1619_1` refers to replicate 1 from random point 19 in the 2016 burn block.
